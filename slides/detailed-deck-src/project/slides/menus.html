In all of these the two unseeded last filters of the Mass90/Mass95 sequence carry l1EGCand = hltEgammaCandidatesUnseeded and saveTags = True, and the ordered list of the sixteen e/gamma filters of the sequence is identical (same MD5). From 2023 the Diphoton30_18 filters were renamed, so its two NanoAOD bits (18, 19) match nothing in those menus, a separate stale-name problem.

The frozen menus are the ones used for Monte Carlo (2022v12 and 2022v14 for Summer22 and Summer22EE, 2023v12 for Summer23 and Summer23BPix, 2024v14 for Summer24, as reported by the production configurations); online data menus evolve during a year but keep the filter names.

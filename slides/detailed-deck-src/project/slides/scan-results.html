Same mechanism as the diphoton bits: only Photon bit 8, whose filter saves HT objects. Electron and Muon have no collection mismatch in any version or menu.

Stale names are a documentation problem: one bit list serves the 2022 to 2026 menus, so bits for renamed or retired filters stay documented but silently empty for later years.

Numbers are for the master configuration against the frozen 2024 v1.4 and 2025 v1.3 menus; the v12, v13 and v15 configurations give the same picture for their own bit lists. L1-seed entries (HLTL1TSeed modules) exist in every menu and are counted as fine.

PR 41827 (merged 8 June 2023) added the unseeded CaloIdL_MW electron bit and "added the collection hltEgammaCandidatesUnseeded, which are the objects on which the selection of the unseeded trigger leg is made". The photon diphoton bits had been added four months earlier by PR 40711 without that change, and nobody came back to it.

The electron precedent is the strongest evidence: the identical construct has been in production since NanoAOD v13 and its unseeded electron bit is populated, which also proves that the unseeded objects are present in MiniAOD and reach the producer.

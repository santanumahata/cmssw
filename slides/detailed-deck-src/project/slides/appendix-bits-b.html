Long module names are broken over two lines for display only. Decimal values of the dead bits: 65536, 131072, 524288 (v15 and master); 16384, 32768, 131072 (v12 to v14).

Bits 18 and 19 name the 2018 and 2022 filters of the Diphoton30_18 paths; from 2023 those filters are called hltEG30Iso60b40eCaloId11b32eHE10b9eR9Id50b90eEcalIsoFilter and hltDiEG18TrackIso60Iso60b40eCaloId11b32eHE10b9eR9Id50b90eTrackIsoUnseededFilter, so the two bits match nothing for 2023 and later data.

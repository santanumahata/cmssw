Same mechanism elsewhere

Only in the Photon list: the three unseeded diphoton bits and bit 8, whose filter saves HT objects. Electron and Muon have no collection mismatch. Two whole tables were empty by configuration: MET in every version since v9, and BoostedTau in v12 to v14, whose selection required a filter name ending in ParticleNetTauTau while the menus end in 0p30 or Tag0p03 (relaxed in 15_0_2).

Stale names are the common case

One bit list serves the 2022 to 2026 menus, so bits for filters that were renamed or retired stay documented but silently empty for later years, and bits added for new filters are empty for earlier years. That is a different, documentation-level problem; the photon one is a code defect that no menu can cure.

Method: every mksel entry of triggerObjects_cff.py (master, v15, v13 and v12 versions) was resolved against the frozen 2022 v1.2 and v1.4, 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026 menus plus the development GRun menus. For each matched filter module the collection its objects come from was read from the menu (l1EGCand, inputTag, CandTag, originTag and so on) and compared with the coll() terms of the selection. The scanner is in the notes repository as scripts/deadbits.py.
